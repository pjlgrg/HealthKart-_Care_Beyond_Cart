Frequently Asked Questions

How does HealthKart help?

HealthKart lets you browse medicines, wellness kits, and nutrition products online. Place your order, share prescriptions if needed, and we deliver everything directly to your door.

Do you sell prescription medicines?

Yes, we stock authentic prescription medicines. Upload a valid prescription at checkout and our pharmacist team verifies it before dispatch.

Can I speak to a pharmacist?

Absolutely. Use the “Consult a Pharmacist” option on our site or write to care@healthkart.com and our licensed experts will call you back.

Are your products genuine?

Every product is sourced directly from certified manufacturers and cold-chain partners. Tamper-proof packaging ensures the order reaches you safely.
