Our Pharmacy Services

We help you get the right medicines and health essentials at home, with simple, safe and clear support at every step.

💊

Online Medicine Ordering

Search and order genuine medicines from trusted brands and nearby stores, without standing in long lines.

  • Easy search by name or symptom
  • Clear details on dose and use
  • Simple, secure checkout
  • Order history in one place
📦

Home Delivery

Get your health essentials delivered to your doorstep, so you can focus on feeling better, not travelling.

  • Fast delivery in many pin codes
  • Safe and sealed packing
  • Delivery updates by message
  • Simple return and support
📑

Prescription Upload

Upload a clear photo of your doctor’s prescription and we help you get the right medicines as written.

  • Easy photo upload from phone
  • Check by licensed pharmacists
  • No change without your approval
  • Secure storage of your files
🔁

Refill Reminders

Never run out of important medicines. We remind you gently when it is time to refill your regular dose.

  • Set reminders for any medicine
  • Simple on/off control by you
  • Timely alerts on your device
  • Quick re-order from last cart
